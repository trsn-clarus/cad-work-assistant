# Workspace Page Rules

The workspace is the first screen and the primary product surface.

- Prioritize repeated CAD quantity work over onboarding copy.
- Show command, extraction, copy, inspector, table, and status affordances immediately.
- Keep metric cards compact and data-heavy.
- Preserve long drawing filenames with trimming rather than breaking the layout.
- Put recovery hints in activity or error states, not in decorative empty-state illustrations.
